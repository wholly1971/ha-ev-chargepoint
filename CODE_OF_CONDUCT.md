# Code of conduct

EV ChargePoint welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Be respectful, constructive and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information or make personal attacks.
- Keep discussions relevant. Respect boundaries and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Do not post customer data, credentials, private invoices or other sensitive information.

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate contributions, close discussions, warn participants or restrict participation according to the behavior and context.

To report a concern privately, use a contact method published on the maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/wholly1971). If no private channel is listed, open a brief issue asking the maintainer for a private contact method; do not identify affected people or publish the incident details in that issue. For software vulnerabilities, follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).
